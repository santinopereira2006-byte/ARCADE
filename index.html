🎮

5 Consecutive Rounds

Every participant progresses through all 5 rounds in sequence. Scores accumulate continuously into a single cumulative total.

🔒

Locked Submissions

Answers lock immediately upon selection or submission. Once locked, answers cannot be modified under any circumstances.

📵

Strict Integrity: No Devices

No mobile phones, no internet search, and no external reference notes or cheat sheets are permitted during gameplay.

⏱️

Tournament Timer System

Rounds 1–4 feature unpausable countdown clocks. Round 5 drops the clock for pure marksmanship and precision target shooting!

🎯

Retro Blast Rules

Round 5 enters the synthwave target simulator: blast targets (+pts) across 3 waves and avoid detonating hazard bombs (-2 pts)!

🏆

Highest Score Wins

The contestant or team with the highest aggregate score wins. Performance Tiers (S, A, B, C, D) are awarded on the results podium.

⚡

Blackout Code Rules

5 progressive rounds under a single 8-minute global timer. Memorize the code before blackout shutters drop (12s, 10s, 4s, and 10s sprint). 1000 max points!

🎨

Creative Standards

Round 3 Meme Studio entries are evaluated by event judges on Originality, Humor, and Relevance. All content must remain college-appropriate.

⚖️

Sudden Death Tie-Breaker

If tied at tournament conclusion, the organizer launches a Sudden Death high-precision estimation challenge (+20 bonus points).
